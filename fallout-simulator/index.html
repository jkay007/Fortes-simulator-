Educational model only. Radiation falls as t-1.2 after the blast (the Way–Wigner decay law behind the 7–10 rule), which holds from roughly 15 minutes to 6 months. All fallout lands at the moment it arrives; in reality it builds up over hours, and rain can concentrate it. Doses treat 1 roentgen as 1 rem. Protection factors are approximate: a ground-floor room about 3, a basement about 20, earth cover from a halving thickness of 12 cm, and Fortes capped at 100,000 to match the Nuclear simulator. Beyond a few metres of earth the limit is the entrance and the air supply, not the rock. The 5 rem trip limit is the annual limit for US radiation workers. None of this is a Fortes guarantee, and real shelters need real dosimeters. Follow local authorities in an emergency: US guidance is to get inside, stay inside, and stay tuned, for at least 24 hours.
