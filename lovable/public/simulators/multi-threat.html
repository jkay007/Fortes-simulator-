    Educational scenario models, not forecasts. The pandemic uses a simple SEIR model with a 5-day incubation and 7-day infectious period and no lockdown or vaccine. Power restoration is a smooth curve around the stated median for each cause. The chemical plume is a steady block of gas passing at 15 km/h; indoor air follows outdoor air at the stated air-change rate. Supplies run down at a steady rate. Timings of knock-on effects at home are typical published estimates and vary by area. Fortes protection figures are illustrative, not a Fortes guarantee.
