    What each depth survives

    Earth against fallout

    Fallout is radioactive dust. The danger is the gamma radiation it gives off, and the simplest shield is mass. Every 10 cm or so of packed earth halves the radiation that gets through. Stack the halvings and the numbers fall away fast.

    That is why even the shallowest Fortes shelter, at 30 metres, is effectively sealed off from fallout. Depth beyond that is about surviving a direct hit, not radiation.

    A brick house cuts fallout radiation only about 3 times. A cellar about 10 to 20 times.

Crater depth scales with yield to the power 0.294 (Glasstone & Dolan, The Effects of Nuclear Weapons, dry soil and soft rock), and twice the crater depth is treated as the zone of crushing and cracking, the same model used by every Fortes simulator. Hard rock makes smaller craters; wet soil larger ones. Weapon yields are public figures rounded for illustration. Fallout shielding uses a halving thickness of about 10 cm of packed earth.
