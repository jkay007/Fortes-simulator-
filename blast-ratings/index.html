What a rating covers

A blast rating is the peak overpressure a structure is designed to take. The blast wave travels through the air, so for a deep shelter what meets it is everything at the surface: the entrance, the blast doors and the air intakes. The rooms below are protected by the earth above them, which is a separate question of depth and cratering. The Depth Explorer covers that part.

    Educational model only. Peak overpressure uses the same scaled curves as the FORTES Blast and Nuclear simulators: Glasstone & Dolan surface-burst curves for a ground burst, and for an airburst a burst at the height that does the most damage, calibrated to the OTA report The Effects of Nuclear War (1 Mt: 5 psi at 7.0 km, 1 psi at 18.6 km). In this model the airburst's pressure on the ground peaks at about 80 psi; a lower burst would push it higher. Real pressures depend on terrain, buildings and the exact height of the burst. Tier ratings, depths and names are the published FORTES figures; ratings are subject to final site engineering. Not a FORTES guarantee.
