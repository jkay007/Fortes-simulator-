      Educational model only. Direct-hit survival uses Glasstone & Dolan crater scaling for a ground burst directly above the shelter and treats twice the crater depth as the damage zone, the same rule as the Nuclear simulator. It ignores geology, burst height, the structure of the shelter, the entrance and anything off-axis, and real ground shock depends on the soil and rock at each site. The fallout protection factor uses a 12 cm halving thickness for earth and is capped at 100,000, as in the Nuclear simulator. Reference depths: Hampstead station, 58.5 m, the deepest on the London Underground; Arsenalna station in Kyiv, 105.5 m, listed as the deepest metro station in the world; basement depth is a typical figure. Systems named in the table are those listed at fortesbunkers.com. Depths, survivable yields and protection figures are illustrative, not a Fortes guarantee.
