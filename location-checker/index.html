Educational model only. The target list is an illustrative set of military sites, cities and industry drawn from public Cold War civil-defence exercises and open-source analysis; it is not intelligence and no one knows a real target plan. Military sites and industry are modelled as ground bursts, cities as airbursts, with a steady 24 km/h wind over flat terrain. Wind frequencies are rounded UK averages. Blast, heat and radiation use the same cube-root scaling and fallout plume as the Fortes nuclear simulator (Glasstone & Dolan; OTA, The Effects of Nuclear War). Shelter depths and protection figures are illustrative, not a Fortes guarantee.
