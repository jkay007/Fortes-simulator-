What is happening

    Three depths. One standard.

    Overhead and near-miss figures are engineering estimates for a surface burst in dry soil, using the Glasstone & Dolan crater scaling and treating twice the crater depth as the damage zone. Rock performs better. Your actual rating depends on the geology at your site and the Fortes design for it.

    How Fortes keeps members alive

    Blast

    Earth overburden and blast doors

    Tens of metres of earth and rock absorb the shock. Even a large airburst over a city peaks at around 80 psi at ground level, a fraction of what the overburden carries.

    Radiation

    Depth as shielding

    Every 10 cm or so of packed earth halves gamma radiation. At 30 m that happens around 300 times, so fallout above cannot be measured below.

    Air

    NBC filtration

    Intake air passes through particle and activated-carbon filters, and the shelter is held at overpressure, so fallout dust, smoke and chemical agents cannot leak in.

    Water

    Stored and purified water

    Members never queue at a broken tap or drink from a contaminated river. Water is stored and treated inside the sealed envelope.

    Food

    12+ months sustained

    Long-life food for the full household, sized to the stay. Members are not competing for empty shelves while the world's harvests fail.

    Power and warmth

    Off-grid systems

    Independent power keeps light, air and comms running when the grid is gone. Below about 10 m the ground holds a steady temperature all year, whatever the sky above is doing.

    The facts behind the simulation

    Nuclear warheads in the worldAbout 12,241 (January 2025), about 3,912 deployed on missiles and aircraft.FAS 2025
    Warning timeAbout 30 minutes for an intercontinental missile, 10 to 15 minutes from a submarine.Union of Concerned Scientists
    Blast, heat, initial radiationCalibrated to Hiroshima, the OTA 1 Mt reference case and Glasstone fireball data (all within 11%).OTA 1979, Glasstone & Dolan
    FalloutPlume calibrated to Castle Bravo doses on Rongelap. Decay follows the 7–10 rule.Glasstone & Dolan
    FamineLimited war (about 100 warheads): global food calories fall 7%. Large regional war: more than 2 billion deaths. US–Russia war: more than 5 billion deaths; calories fall about 90% three to four years on.Xia et al., Nature Food 2022
    Nuclear winterSmoke from burning cities blocks sunlight. The largest scenario cools the planet by around 7 to 8 °C for several years.Robock et al. 2007
    Bunker-busting bombsGBU-28 penetrates about 30 m of earth. GBU-57 is reported to reach about 60 m.US Air Force, public reports
    Survival without suppliesAbout 3 days without water, about 3 weeks without food.General medical guidance
    Home supplies adviceUK government: at least 3 days. US Ready.gov: up to 2 weeks.gov.uk, ready.gov
